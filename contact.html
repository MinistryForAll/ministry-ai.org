Support this work

MinistryAI is free to every church that adopts it. That is possible because donors carry the cost — the engineering, the corpus work with each congregation, and the onboarding and training that follow.

Gifts to MinistryAI are tax-deductible in the United States to the extent allowed by law. We are a 501(c)(3) nonprofit corporation, EIN 41-4049715. If you would like to give, or to talk first about where a gift would go, write to us and we will send you the details.
