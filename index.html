Our Mission

Equipping Shepherds.
Strengthening the Sheep.

MinistryAI exists out of obedience to Jesus Christ — to his command to love God and neighbor and to make disciples of all nations. From this conviction flows our belief that AI, used wisely, can help the body of Christ proclaim the Gospel more broadly and care for its people more fully; used carelessly, it erodes the embodied, relational ministry at the heart of congregational life.

We exist to build a trusted, reliable AI platform that serves the church in two directions.

A 501(c)(3) nonprofit, funded by donors who share our vision — built to serve your sacred calling, never to replace it.

For pastors and leaders — dependable tools to better listen to, care for, and disciple their congregations.

For members and seekers — a safe, private place to bring questions and struggles, and be drawn toward the pastors and shepherds who lead them to Christ and his church.

How We Protect Members

When Someone Shares Vulnerably,
They Are Safe Here

Many people hesitate to seek pastoral care because they fear exposure. MinistryAI is designed to remove that barrier — guarding what is sacred while giving leadership the insight to shepherd well.

Four privacy tiers — designed for the member to choose at the point of submission

Fully Anonymous

Designed to strip identifying details and contribute only to aggregate patterns. Leadership sees themes; no one is meant to be traceable to an individual.

Confidential Pastoral Routing

Sent directly to a designated pastoral care provider with identity intact, and excluded from aggregate analysis. Between the individual and their pastor.

Identified but Sanitized

A specific pastor knows who they are, while a sanitized version informs broader pastoral awareness. Personal attention alongside collective insight.

Full Transparency

For non-sensitive matters — theological questions, teaching feedback, curiosities — members may choose full visibility.

These are product design commitments. For how we handle data as an organization, read our Privacy Policy.
