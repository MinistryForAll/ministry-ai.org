The Technology

How the Platform Is Built

Relationship-aware AI orchestration, grounded in your materials and designed to defer to your shepherds.

Private Corpus

Grounded in a vetted body of your church's materials — doctrine, liturgy, approved sermons — so guidance reflects your teaching, not a borrowed theology.

Closed-Corpus RAG

Each agent draws only from your approved materials, with citations you can verify. Designed to keep answers grounded, not guessing.

Relational Deferral

When someone is in real distress, the system is designed to step back and point them to a pastor or care-team member. Presence over precision.

Guardrails & Consent

We are developing safeguards to keep each interaction theologically sound and pastorally appropriate, with explicit opt-in for sensitive information.

Private & Secure

When Someone Shares Vulnerably,
They Are Safe Here

Many people hesitate to seek pastoral care because they fear exposure. MinistryAI is designed to remove that barrier — guarding what is sacred while giving leadership the insight to shepherd well.

Four privacy tiers — designed for the member to choose at the point of submission

Fully Anonymous

Designed to strip identifying details and contribute only to aggregate patterns. Leadership sees themes; no one is meant to be traceable to an individual.

Confidential Pastoral Routing

Sent directly to a designated pastoral care provider with identity intact, and excluded from aggregate analysis. Between the individual and their pastor.

Identified but Sanitized

A specific pastor knows who they are, while a sanitized version informs broader pastoral awareness. Personal attention alongside collective insight.

Full Transparency

For non-sensitive matters — theological questions, teaching feedback, curiosities — members may choose full visibility.
